# Pay in your own words

A chat assistant for paying bills and sending money in Iraqi Arabic.

Built for the **Agentic AI Challenge Iraq**, Topic 04: Bill Pay and Transfer Agent.

## Why

Many people find wallet apps hard to use. They pay the same few bills and send money to family regularly, but the app expects them to know exactly where to tap and what to type. So they often hand the phone to someone else or visit an agent shop.

This project explores a simpler way: the user says what they want, in their own words, and stays in control of every payment.
